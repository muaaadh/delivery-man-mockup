Questions

Anything else, message us on WhatsApp or Viber.

How do I pay?

By bank transfer to our Bank of Maldives or Maldives Islamic Bank account, using your order code as the reference. Upload a screenshot of the slip on the payment page and we confirm it, usually in about 30 minutes.

How long does a delivery take?

Usually within 60 to 90 minutes in Malé and Hulhumalé when you choose "as soon as possible". You can also pick a 2-hour window later today or another day.

How is the price worked out?

By size: bag, box or XL. Within one island you pay the lower figure; when we cross the Sinamalé Bridge you pay the higher one. Airport and cargo terminal runs add a fixed fee. You see the full price before you send the request.

My package is big or heavy. Can you still take it?

Yes. Tick "Too big or heavy for a bike" when you request. We confirm the vehicle charge with you first and you only pay once you have the final price.

How does shop & deliver work?

You send a list and a budget and pay the budget plus the shopping fee up front. We show you the receipt; if it comes in under budget we refund the difference, if it is over we ask before paying more.

Do you go to Villimalé or other islands?

Villimalé by ferry, and other islands through the cargo boats at Malé harbour. These are quoted before pickup.

What don't you carry?

We don't carry items prohibited under Maldives law or cash. Fragile items travel at the sender's risk unless they are boxed.

Can I cancel?

Before you pay, cancel from your tracking page. After payment, message us and we will sort it out, including a refund if the rider has not been sent yet.
