Questions.

Anything else, message us on WhatsApp or Viber.

How do I pay?

By bank transfer to our Bank of Maldives or Maldives Islamic Bank account, after delivery: your invoice is on the tracking page. If you prefer, pay upfront once we confirm the price. Upload a screenshot of the slip and we verify it, usually in about 30 minutes.

How long does a delivery take?

Usually within 60 to 90 minutes in Malé and Hulhumalé on a normal order. Choose Express to jump the queue (we confirm it first), or an Advance order to book the collection and delivery days ahead.

How is the price worked out?

By size: bag MVR 35–45, box MVR 45–60, XL-size package MVR 60++. Within one island you pay the lower figure; when we cross the Sinamalé Bridge you pay the higher one. Maldivian Cargo deliveries add a cargo fee of MVR 20, and airport deliveries an airport fee of MVR 40; boat and home deliveries have no extra fee. No measuring: you choose the size and can add a photo, and our team confirms the exact price after checking it, or the driver's photo at collection.

Can you collect my Redbox, Pikpost or post office parcel?

Yes. Choose Postal & courier, pick the carrier and add the collection code or tracking number from your SMS. For the post office without a code, send your ID details to the post collections desk and we do the rest. DHL needs the collection document, which you upload with the request.

Do you deliver to the airport and resort counters?

Yes. Choose Airport delivery and we hand it to a resort counter or a meeting point at Velana Airport. Add the receiver, the resort or destination, and the resort speedboat and its departure time if there is one. The airport fee of MVR 40 is added to the price.

My package is big or heavy. Can you still take it?

Yes. Choose XL-size package. With more than one box or more than one XL package you can ask for an extra vehicle; our team confirms the vehicle charge before you pay. If a package does not match the size you chose, our team changes it and you see the new price first.

How does Shop & Deliver work?

Tell us the shop or supplier, list each item with its details, quantity and any special instructions, and set a budget. You pay the budget, the shopping fee and the delivery charge upfront; the charges are shown before you send. We show you the receipt; if it comes in under budget we refund the difference, if it is over we ask before paying more.

Can you collect something I already paid for in a shop?

Yes. Choose Pick & Deliver, then Shop or godown collection. Give us the shop or godown and upload the invoice or the quotation with your payment slip, and we collect it for you.

Do you go to Villimalé or other islands?

Villimalé by ferry. For other islands, choose Boat, ferry or speedboat: we hand the package to the vessel at the harbour or jetty, with the boat name, departure time and receiver on the label. Or choose Maldivian Cargo: we take it to the Maldivian Cargo counter at the main bus terminal, and a cargo fee of MVR 20 applies only to this option.

Do you deliver for businesses?

Yes. Request a business account with a photo of a typical package; we use it to agree your package size categories and a fixed rate for each. Send orders one at a time or in bulk from a spreadsheet, and we collect cash on delivery from your customers and pay it to you.

What don't you carry?

We don't carry items prohibited under Maldives law or cash. Fragile items travel at the sender's risk unless they are boxed.

Can I cancel?

Yes, until the package is collected: ask from your tracking page and our team confirms it. If you already paid, we refund you.
