# Updates of 09/11/2026 (client points of 06 to 09/11)

**Bulk orders: more packages for one receiver.** A receiver with more bags no longer has to be entered again as a new receiver. In the
business portal's bulk order each card is a receiver ("Receiver 1, 2…", "Add a receiver" below) with "Add another package": each
package has its agreed size and its contents. The receiver is one order with a label per package, priced at each package's agreed rate
(`row.more`, `store.batchOrder`). Spreadsheet lines for the same receiver (Excel import and the office's New bulk order: same name,
number, delivery method and address) become one order with a package per line; their cash to collect adds up and their references are
kept (`sameReceiver` in `store.createBatch`). The bulk pages, the driver's bulk collection, the collector's message and the labels
count packages.

**Taken out: "Add a package" on a bulk order already made** (05/11/2026, `store.addToBatch`): no change was wanted there.

**Photos on the link we send.** The link of a bulk package now shows the driver's photo of the bulk collection (it belongs to the bulk
order, not to one package; `api/track.js` also reads the files the order points to that belong to no order). On the live service every
photo on the link showed as a grey "PDF" tile, because the page drew a picture only for a photo kept inside the page and live photos
are signed links. It shows the picture now, for every order, and opens it in a new tab.

**Shop & Deliver: a sample photo per item.** "Add a sample photo" under each item to buy, optional, on the customer's form and the
office's new order (`details.items[].photoId`, file kind `item_photo`). The driver has "Photo: <item>" with the job's files, the office
sees it next to the item and among the order's photos, and the link we send shows it.

**Numbers from abroad.** A customer's own number (the request form, the office's new order, the e-store) may be a foreign WhatsApp or
Viber number: + (or 00) and the country code, for example +44 7700 900123. A number of 7 digits without a country code is still read as
Maldivian, so local numbers are entered as before. Receiver and contact numbers already took numbers from abroad.

Tests: demo `tools/_scratch/r11-client.js`; live `tools/supabase/test-r11.js`, test-live section 3.

## Afternoon (client points of 09/11, 1:40 PM)

**Business order: "Add another order".** On the office's New order → For a business account, "Add another order" under the card adds
the next receiver as an order of its own (like "Add a stop" on a customer order). The cards are "Order 1, 2…", each with its receiver,
delivery, size, packages, cash to collect and extra vehicle, and can be removed; they share the account, the collection and the
timing. The price lists each order and their total; Create order books them one by one (an order booked before a failure leaves the
form, so trying again does not book it twice). The business portal keeps Bulk for several receivers.

**A customer's email linked to their number.** Customers → Edit (name, email, how we send updates). On the live service customers sign
in with their email: with it on their record, signing in with that email shows the orders of that number (before, the sign-up said
"This mobile number already has orders under another email… call us to add it", and the office had no way to add it). A new email
moves the sign-in to it; the activity log says who changed it (`store.editCustomer`, office only). The live sign-up also takes a
mobile from abroad (+ and the country code).

**"Order received by" sets "Notify by".** On the office's customer order, WhatsApp and Viber set "Notify by" to the same (also for a
known customer); Phone and Walk-in keep the choice, and it can still be changed in Customer details.

Tests: demo `tools/_scratch/r12-client.js`; live `tools/supabase/test-r12.js`.

## Evening (client, 09/11: a second receiver on the business order; "the website is slow")

**Business portal: "Add another order".** The business portal's New order has the same button as the office's business order: "Add
another order" under the card adds the next receiver as an order of its own, from the same pickup and with the same service. The cards
become "Order 1, 2…" (up to 10), each with its receiver, delivery, size, packages, fragile, cash to collect and extra vehicle, and can be
removed. The summary lists each order and the total; Book delivery books them one by one (an order booked before a failure leaves the
form, so trying again does not book it twice) and ends on one confirmation with every code, a Track link each and "Print N labels" for
all of them. One card looks and works as before. Bulk stays for long lists and spreadsheets.

**Faster on the live service.** Measured on mrdeliveryman.com over the last days: a save (any button that changes something) took
0.9 s on average and up to 15 s, of which about 0.3 s went to asking Supabase whether the sign-in is valid and another round trip to
reading who the person is before reading the data. Now the sign-in is checked on our server against the project's public signing key
(read once, then kept), and who the person is loads at the same time as the data: two waits for the database per save instead of four.
Opening a page read the orders' details, files and photo links in many small requests one after another (the office's copy: one per
100 orders, then one per 100 photos). They now go out at the same time, and the photo links are made once the page shows. In a test
with 620 orders and 1,160 photos and 120 ms per request, the office page opened in 2.4 s instead of 5.3 s, and the gap grows with the
orders. Inside the page, each update no longer copies all the orders as text (13 ms each with 620 orders, more on slower computers), an
update that changes nothing (the echo of your own save) no longer redraws the page, and the counts in the menu are worked out again only
when orders, requests, leave or bulk orders change, not on every rider position.

Tests: demo `tools/_scratch/r13-client.js`; live `tools/supabase/test-r13.js` (the API behind a stand-in at 100 ms per call), test-api,
test-live.
