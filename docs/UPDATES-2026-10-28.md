# Updates of 28/10/2026 (client screenshots, MDM-1001 and MDM-1002)

**A driver before the price.** MDM-1001 (a home collection with a shop stop, no photo) could not get a driver until the price
was confirmed, although its banner said "confirm now or after collection". Every request can now be given a driver first:
`store.assignDriver` on a `requested` order whose price is not set turns on `pricing.afterCollection`, so the order follows the
price-after-collection flow (`MDM.priceToFinalise` once a collection is done, no invoice until `confirmPrice`). The public event
reads "Driver assigned: X. We confirm your price after collection". In the office, the "Confirm the price" banner and the drawer
footer show the driver picker and "Assign driver" next to "Confirm price"; the request stays in "Price to confirm" until then.
An order that pays upfront (shopping) asks for the payment when the price is confirmed.

**Correcting the price after delivery.** MDM-1002: the driver changed Bag to Box at collection and the confirmed MVR 45 became
MVR 55 on the invoice. A delivered order whose payment is still `pending`, `requested` or `received` is `correctable`
(admin/orders.js): Pricing keeps "Change confirmed price", "Add extra vehicle charge" and "Add adjustment", and each package keeps
"Change category". A paid order, or a business order already on a weekly invoice, cannot change. When the total of a delivered
order with an invoice out changes, the store adds the public event `invoice_updated` "Invoice updated: MVR X" (customer
notification "Invoice updated"; hidden from receivers like the other money events). `confirmPrice` no longer asks a customer
who already sent a transfer slip (`received`) to pay again.
