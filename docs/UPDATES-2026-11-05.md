# Updates of 05/11/2026 (client list of 04 and 05/11)

**The office page went stale (live fix).** "This order can no longer be cancelled" on MDM-1290 and "This stop is already done" on
MDM-1308 were refused by the server (409 at 23:02 and 00:02) because the office page still showed an old copy of the order. Supabase
closes a page's Realtime channel when the sign-in it joined with runs out (an hour; a background tab does not refresh its sign-in),
and a closed channel never joins again by itself. `js/remote.js` now replaces a closed or failed channel with a new one on a fresh
sign-in, catches up on every join, when the page comes back into view or online, every two minutes, and after the API refuses a
change, so the page shows where the order is. test-live section 13. Also fixed from the logs: a removed file, address or collection
point by someone signed in with an email failed with a server error (`server/handler.js`), and a job a driver no longer has (handed to
the office, given to someone else) leaves their phone.

**Reassign at every stage.** On the way and Arrived have Reassign too (`store.assignDriver`: after Arrived the order is on the way
again with the new driver). The new driver is told.

**Handed over to the office.** A driver who brings a collected package to the office taps "Handed to the office" (an optional note)
instead of reporting a problem (`store.handToOffice`): the order stays collected, without a driver (`o.atOffice`), and leaves their
list. The office sees it in the "At the office" queue and on the order, and gives it to a driver for the delivery.

**Change the delivery method.** "Change delivery method" on the order (two steps: the method, then its details) moves every package
to the new delivery, Maldivian Cargo to home delivery or any other way round (`store.changeDeliveryMethod`); the cargo and airport fees
follow the method, the stops still to do move, the driver is told.

**Bulk orders: Add a package.** The office (bulk order page) and the business (its own bulk order) add packages to a bulk order
already made (`store.addToBatch`), at the account's agreed rates, waiting for the collection like the others.

**Overview.** No daily sales amount.

**Reports.** By driver: collected, delivered, failed, packages collected and delivered, each counted for the driver who did it.
Sales and expenses (`MDM.salesOf`): sales (delivery charge, shopping fee, airport fee, Maldivian Cargo fee, charges billed on) and
expenses (naalu, vehicle, packing, Maldivian Cargo charges, shopping item cost, other), each kind on or off, gross sales, expenses and
net for the orders delivered in the period, by day and by order, with CSV.

**Customers.** General customers and business customers, a search, the email, and Export (CSV) of either list.

**Customer service sign-in.** A role of its own (`cs`, Customer service): orders, bulk orders, live map, customers, business and
notifications, no money pages. On the live service an admin gives any office employee a sign-in from Team and HR → Sign-in (an email
and a first password, `api/staff-login.js`); demo user `care`.

**Tracking.** The link we send (with its secret) shows the whole order, without signing in: the proof of collection and delivery
photos, the customer and delivery details, the price and payment. Tracking by the order number alone (main page) shows the status
and its steps, and the payment while one is due; "Sign in to see the proof of delivery and the order details".

**Business sign-in.** A business sign-in that is not on a business account yet (a request still waiting, or the account has another
email) no longer signs out the person's customer sign-in on the same email; it says what to do.

**Jetties and post.** Rates → Jetties and post: the office edits the jetties and harbours, the Pikpost lockers, the post offices and
the postal collection addresses (`settings.ops.jetties | pikpost | postOffices | carrierAddresses`, `MDM.applyPlaces`).

**Customer emails.** Order confirmed, collected, delivered and payment due go to the email on the order (not bulk packages), each on
or off in Settings → Notifications. On the live service `api/store.js` sends them through Resend (`RESEND_API_KEY`) and marks them
sent or failed in the notifications log; without the key they stay queued.

**A driver as a customer.** A driver may order as a customer with the same sign-in (the sign-up step links the customer record).

Tests: demo `tools/_scratch/r10-store.js`, `r10-orders.js`, `r10-reports.js`, `r10-views.js`, `r10-driver-track.js`; live
`tools/supabase/test-r10.js`, test-live sections 3 and 13.
