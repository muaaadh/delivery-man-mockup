# Updates of 20/11/2026 (client points of 18 to 20/11/2026)

Seventeen points from the client's WhatsApp list. The biggest change is how a business pays (points 3 and 4); the rest are forms, the
driver's screens and the tracking page.

## Payments

**A customer pays only after the office asks.** "For payments to be shown in the payment links … after it's completed finalized by the
operator (to avoid missing any naalu/pickup fee or any other), else sometimes customer pays before the naalu is being entered."

- A delivery no longer asks for its payment by itself. The delivered order stays in the board's Delivery column with **Request
  payment** (or **Finalise price** first, when the price was left for after collection). The operator checks naalu and other charges,
  then requests payment, and only then does the tracking link, the payment page and "My orders" offer "Pay now".
- Until then the tracking page says **"We are finalising your charges"**: the amount to pay shows there shortly.
- Not changed: shopping and other orders paid upfront still ask for payment when their price is confirmed.

**A business pays after each delivery, or by the 14th and 28th.** "Keep all amounts due after each delivery is completed or the item is
returned (returned packages also will have 1 time delivery charge), payment due in every 14 days, but they can pay after each
delivery. Due dates for business invoices to be set as 14th of each month & 28th of each month."

- A business delivery reads **On account** until it is delivered, or returned to the sender. The office then presses **Finalise
  charges** (the order's footer, its Payment section, or "Finalise" on the board card). That sets the amount, and the due date: the
  next 14th or 28th.
- From then the business sees it under **Payments** in its portal: "Amount due", the due date, each delivery with **Pay**. It can pay
  one delivery at once (bank transfer and slip, as a customer does) or wait for the invoice.
- A package returned to the sender is charged its delivery once and is finalised the same way ("Returned" on the board).
- The office's account page shows "Amount due" for the business and its invoice terms, and **Finalise N deliveries**: every
  finished delivery of that business finalised in one go, for a business with many deliveries a day.
- Deliveries completed before 20/11/2026 are left as they were (billed by their invoice): they do not ask to be finalised.

**Business invoices.**

- An invoice lists what is delivered or returned and **not paid yet**; a delivery the business already paid by itself is left off. It
  is due on the next 14th or 28th. "Everything not paid so far" makes one invoice for all of it, whatever the week.
- A generated invoice has **no "Pay now" for the public**: only the business (signed in) and the office see how to pay it.
- **Transfer slip on an invoice**: the business uploads it from Payments in its portal or from the invoice ("Upload slip"); the office
  can attach one when it marks the invoice as paid. The invoice reads "Slip received" until the office presses **Verify** (or
  **Reject** with a reason, and the business uploads another).
- **Marking an invoice as paid pays its orders**: each delivery on it becomes Paid ("Paid with invoice INV-…"), so the tracking link
  and the portal no longer show it as due.
- Rates → "Business invoices due" states the 14th and 28th.

## Orders and forms

- **Velana International Airport** is an area in the business order form (the office's and the portal's), with the airport fee.
- **Boat collection: the arrival time may be left empty** ("Leave empty if not known yet"). The order, the driver's job and the
  tracking page read "time to be confirmed".
- **"What is inside?" is optional for the office.** A customer still fills it in.
- **A second collection address needs its contact number.**
- **A shop at a second stop has its items**: name, details and quantity for each, with "Add item", as the first shop has.
- **Post office.** Always: owner full name, owner contact number, shipping address. The tracking number and the collection code are
  optional. Without a collection code the form says that both sides of the owner's ID card go to our post collections numbers once a
  driver is assigned, with a tick to agree; the tracking page repeats it, and says "send them now" once a driver is assigned. The
  numbers are in Rates → "Post collections numbers" (several, separated by commas).
- **Package sizes of a business.** Each size (Small, Medium, Large…) can carry a **description** for that business, typed in "Edit
  rates" and shown to the business under "Your rates". A business asking for an account chooses a **size for each package photo** it
  sends. Afterwards it can **Add a package type** from its portal (photo, size if known, what it is): the office is told, and can also
  add or remove package photos on the account page.

## The office

- **Viber** beside Call · WhatsApp · SMS on an order's contact numbers.
- **Dispatch** is in the brand colour, so an assigned order that still has to be dispatched stands out.
- **Add package** on an order (Packages section): size, description, fragile, optional photo. The price follows.
- **Scan** on the Orders page opens the camera: scan a label's QR code and the order opens. A photo of the label, or the number typed
  in, works too.
- **Send update.** The first message of an order carries the link and the three lines the client wrote ("Track your delivery and view
  the POD photo through the link…"). Later ones are the order number and the update only. The payment message keeps its link.

## The driver

- **Scan a label** at the top of the driver's jobs: it opens that job, and when the package is out for delivery it opens the
  "Delivered" step with the photo.
- **Add package** on a job, when the customer hands over one more at the collection: size, description, optional photo.
- **Collecting several packages**: one photo of them together, and a tick for each package. A photo of each package is optional.
- **Add receipt** on a shopping job, and two more receipts in the "Collected" step: a second or third shop's bill, each with its
  amount and photo.

## The tracking page

- **Photos under their stop**: the proof of collection under the collection (with its time), a shop's receipts under the shop, the
  proof of delivery under the delivery (with the time and who received it).
- **A delivery that could not be completed** shows what the driver reported, on the link we share and in the business's portal
  ("Deliveries with a problem"). The business gets a notification, and a customer with an email gets the "Delivery problem" email
  (Settings → Notifications).
- A business delivery without cash on delivery no longer shows "Cash to pay the driver: Nothing to pay".

## In the data

- `payment.dueAt` on a business order once its charges are finalised; `MDM.completedAt(order)`, `MDM.toFinalise(order)`,
  `MDM.dueDate(at)`, `MDM.DUES_FROM`.
- `store.addPackage(orderId, { size | tier, description, fragile, photoId, stopId })`, `store.addReceipt(orderId, { shop, amount,
  note, photoId })`.
- `store.createInvoice(accountId, period, { by, except })`, `store.markInvoicePaid(invoiceId, { paidAt, reference, slip })`,
  `store.submitInvoicePayment(invoiceId, { slip, bank, payerName, reference, paidAmount })`, `store.rejectInvoicePayment(invoiceId,
  { reason })`; `MDM.invoiceState(invoice)` reads "received" from `invoice.payment` (the stored status stays draft, sent or paid).
- `account.rateNotes: { small: '…' }`, `account.packagePhotos: [{ fileId, tier, note, at, by }]`;
  `store.setBusinessRates(accountId, rates, by, notes)`, `store.addBusinessPackage`, `store.removeBusinessPackage`.
- `settings.ops.postContacts: ['7xxxxxx', …]`; `details.idSent` on a post office order without a code.
- An extra stop at a shop keeps `shop.items: [{ name, details, qty }]` beside its one-line `shop.list`.
- `MDM.ui.scanCode({ title, hint })` → an order number or null (html5-qrcode, loaded when the scanner opens: `docs/CDN.md`).

## On the live service

- Seven new calls go through the API: `addPackage`, `addReceipt`, `markInvoicePaid`, `submitInvoicePayment`,
  `rejectInvoicePayment`, `addBusinessPackage`, `removeBusinessPackage` (`js/remote.js` WRITES, `server/methods.js`,
  `server/permissions.js`).
- Who may: the office all of them. A **driver** adds a package or a receipt to a job assigned to them, with a photo only when they
  took it for that order. A **business** sends the slip for its own invoice (a slip it uploaded) and adds a package type to its own
  account (a package photo it uploaded); marking an invoice paid, rejecting a slip and removing a package photo are the office's.
  A business pays its own finalised delivery; the shared tracking link never pays a business delivery (its receiver holds it).
- Migration `20261120000100_business_dues.sql` replaces `public.mdm_load` (no table changes): `accountDelivered` also brings the
  packages returned to the sender, and the new `invoiceOrders: [invoice id]` brings the orders an invoice lists, so marking it paid
  pays them. Push the migration with the code. If the code is live first, marking an invoice paid still pays its orders (the
  API then reads them in a second load, by their numbers); only the returned packages wait for the migration to show on invoices.
- An invoice's status in the database stays draft, sent or paid; "Slip received" is read from the slip in `invoices.extra.payment`.
- The customer email for a delivery or collection that could not be completed is sent like the other four (`server/mailer.js`,
  Settings → Notifications → "Delivery problem").
- A package photo or a slip that the **office** uploads is readable by the office only (the files rule): a business sees the photos
  and slips it uploaded itself.
- The scanner needs the camera permission (`camera=(self)` is in the site's Permissions-Policy) and loads html5-qrcode from cdnjs.
