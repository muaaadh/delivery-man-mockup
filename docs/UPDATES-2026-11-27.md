# Updates of 27/11/2026 (client points of 25 and 26/11/2026)

## The tracking link and the order form were slow

"Tracking link is still very slow, customers doesn't wait enough to load. They think it doesn't work. And order placing window
takes a while to load as well." The order form also answered "The server did not answer. Try again in a minute."

- The cause was in the database. Each page asks for the photos and documents it may see, and for anyone but our office the rule
  that decides this looked up the order behind each file in the whole table, one by one, every time. Every customer, guest, driver
  and business page did this when it opened, and every driver's phone repeated it every five minutes and each time it was
  unlocked. With every photo added it got slower, and when many of these ran at once the database was too busy to answer the
  tracking link or save an order in time.
- Now the rule asks once which orders the person may see, then checks each file against that answer. On a copy of the database
  the size of the live one, a customer's page went from about 0.6 s of database work to 0.01 s, and so did each driver's refresh.
  Who may see which file is unchanged.
- The tracking link also asks for the order at the same moment as the rest of the page, not after it: about a second sooner on
  a phone.
- Indexes for the "what changed since I last looked" questions the office and the drivers' pages ask every few minutes.
- The office's order form still loads the last 60 days of orders, because it needs them. If it is still slow after this update,
  tell us and we will look at that next.

## "Pay and upload the slip"

"Once it comes to payment, is it possible to change pay and upload the slip. Instead of pay and track?"

- The message for a delivery that is waiting for payment (Send update) now ends with "Pay and upload the slip here:". The link goes
  straight to the payment page, where the slip is uploaded, instead of the tracking page. The payment page links back to tracking.
- The same for Request payment ("Pay by bank transfer and upload the slip here:") and for the "payment due" email, whose button
  reads **Pay and upload the slip**.

- **Scan a label** is beside **Use my GPS** in the driver's card at the top of the page. It is no longer above the jobs.

  passed.

## Business requests from a business that already has an account

"An existing business customer sent a business account request themselves. When we linked it their records were reset, and the
sample photo and collection address from the new request did not come with it."

- Linking only marked the request approved: the request's photos and collection address never reached the account, and an
  account without an email kept none, so nobody could sign in to it. Nothing was lost: every account linked this way still has
  all its orders. A business signed in with an email other than the account's sees no account, which looks like its records are
  gone.
- **Approve** now looks for the business's account by the same mobile number, landline, email or name, and offers to **link** to
  it. With the same number or email it does not open a second account.
- **Link** (new, beside Approve and Decline) links a request to any account you choose. The account keeps its orders, rates,
  invoices and sign-in. It takes the request's package photos and its collection address, added as a collection point, not the
  default. A tick lets the business sign in with the email on the request.
- The two requests waiting: link them once this update is live.
- The requests linked before this update: their photos and collection addresses are added to the accounts, and an account with
  no email takes the one on its request (a one-off repair of the live data). Where a business applied with another email than its
  account's and cannot see its orders, set the account's sign-in email to the one it uses (Business, the account, Edit). Whoever
  already signs in keeps the account.

## Boat collection details can be corrected

"This boat was supposed to arrive to North Harbour in Malé but it went to Hulhumalé. We were able to change the zone but couldn't
edit jetty details."

- **Edit boat details** in a boat collection order: arriving on, the boat's name, the jetty, the arrival date and time, who hands
  it over and their number, and where it comes from.
- A new jetty, or a new boat name, moves the collection there, with the package and the driver's stops still to do. If it moves to
  another island, the message says to check the price. Each change is recorded with who made it.
