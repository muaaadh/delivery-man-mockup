# Web app updates of 17 October 2026: data contract

The client's follow-up of 17/10/2026 (after reviewing the 11/10 build), mapped onto the data layer. It builds on
`docs/UPDATES-2026-10-11.md`; read that first. Store schema is now **4** (`MDM.SCHEMA`), so older demo data reseeds.

## 1. Signed-in customers skip "Your details"

- A signed-in customer (`localStorage mdm:me`) whose customer record has a name, a valid number and `termsAcceptedAt` skips
  step 5 of the request form: the stepper shows Service, Collection, Packages, Delivery, Review ("Step 5 of 5" on the review).
  The account fills the order's customer; "Edit" on the review's "You" group opens step 5 for that request (`draft.editDetails`).
- "When do you want to pay?" moved from step 5 to the review for everyone (`request-pay`). Orders that buy anything (Shop &
  Deliver, or an extra shop stop) are paid upfront and show a note instead.

## 2. Price after collection when there is no package photo

- `createOrder` sets `order.pricing.afterCollection = true` for a pick & deliver request with no customer package photo and no
  extra shop stop. `MDM.priceAfterCollection(o)` is that flag while the price is not confirmed; `MDM.priceToFinalise(o)` adds
  "a collection stop is done" (the proof of collection is in) and the order is not cancelled or returned.
- `MDM.ALLOWED.requested` includes `assigned`: `store.assignDriver` on a `requested` order works only when
  `MDM.priceAfterCollection(o)`; the public event says the price is confirmed from the driver's photo at collection.
- When every collection is done the office gets an `audience: 'operations'` notification "Finalise the price". The operator
  confirms it with `store.confirmPrice` while the package is out for delivery.
- Delivered before the price is confirmed: no invoice goes out (the office is told to finalise); `confirmPrice` on a delivered
  order with a pending payment issues the invoice.
- Orders with a customer photo, shopping orders and orders created already confirmed keep the price-first flow.
  Since 28/10/2026 the office may still assign them a driver first; see `UPDATES-2026-10-28.md`.

## 3. Maldivian Cargo charges on proof of delivery

- New charge type `cargo` ("Maldivian Cargo charges") in `MDM.CHARGE_TYPES` and `MDM.ADJUSTMENT_PRESETS`. It is billable by
  default: the amount on the Maldivian Cargo bill is added to the customer's delivery charges as an adjustment.
- Proof of delivery on a Maldivian Cargo delivery: `setStop(..., { cargoCharge, cargoNote, cargoBillPhotoId })` records it as
  paid by the driver at the delivery stage (so the office reimburses it like naalu). `charge.photoId` holds the bill photo
  (`files` kind `cargo_bill`).
- The MVR 20 Maldivian Cargo fee (our fee) is unchanged and stays its own line.

## 4. "I've read what we carry" moved to sign-up and guest orders

- `customers[].termsAcceptedAt`; `store.upsertCustomer({ termsAcceptedAt })` stores it once. `order.termsAcceptedAt` records
  the tick (the guest's, or the account's sign-up time).
- Request form: a guest (not signed in, or an account without `termsAcceptedAt`) ticks it in step 5 (`request-terms`).
- Customer sign-in: a number with no customer record, or a record without `termsAcceptedAt`, gets a sign-up step (name +
  the terms text + the tick) after the code. The business account request form has the same tick.
- Checkout no longer asks for it.

## 5. Multi collection, delivered to one address

- `order.extraCollections = [{ id, kind: 'collect' | 'shop', address, zone, landmark, contactName, contactPhone, instructions,
  shop: { name, phone, list, budget, unavailable, receiptTotal, receiptPhotoId } | null }]`, up to `MDM.MAX_EXTRA_COLLECTIONS`
  (4). `createOrder({ extraCollections })` cleans them; `MDM.EXTRA_COLLECTION_KINDS` labels the two kinds.
- `buildStops` adds one pickup stop per extra collection after the main one (`collectionId`, `packageIds: []`, `shop: true`
  for a shop). Everything is packed together, so packages and the delivery are unchanged and any delivery method works
  (home, boat, Maldivian Cargo, airport). Collections are numbered only when there is more than one non-shop collection.
- A collection done while others remain moves the order from `arrived` back to `on_the_way` (event `next_collection`).
- Pricing: an extra shop's budget (then its receipt, from `setStop(..., { receiptTotal })` on that stop) adds to
  `totals.budget` and the shopping fee; any extra stop adds the quote reason `stops` and is priced by the team (adjustment
  preset `stop`, "Extra collection"). An extra shop stop makes the order paid upfront.
- Request form: "Add another collection or shop" in step 2 (both Pick & Deliver and Shop & Deliver); "Order again" copies the
  extra stops.
