# Updates of 01/11/2026 (after the training: drivers and operators)

**Driver console.** The packages and the request's own details (the shop's invoice or quotation, the collection code and time, the
tracking number) sit right under "Collect from", before "Deliver to"; only the money recorded stays at the bottom. The action button
has a colour per stage: green to start and collect, yellow once collected, red to deliver (the progress bar follows). Collected
folds the naalu and remarks into one line. Delivered takes "Handed to" from a short list, drops the "recipient confirmed" tick (a
hand-over in person is the confirmation; a photo is needed only when the package is left as instructed) and folds the naalu, packing
and Maldivian Cargo charges into one line, so "Confirm delivery" stays on screen. `MDM.ui.dialog` fields take `fold: 'Title'` for
this. A PDF document opens from a button (it showed as a broken picture), and on the live service a job assigned after the page
loaded reads its files when one is opened (`MDM.remote.file`), instead of "That file is no longer available".

**Office: messages.** "Send update" opens a small dialog with the message and WhatsApp, Viber and SMS to tap, the customer's
channel first: a tap is what lets the browser open the app (Viber did not open). Viber gets the text in its link and on the
clipboard. "Request payment" then opens it with the payment message: the amount and the tracking link to pay.

**Paying without signing in.** The tracking link (the one with its secret) shows the amount due and "Pay"; the payment page works
from it without signing in: the slip is uploaded for that order and the payment sent once. The invoice, photos and personal details
stay for the signed-in owner. On the live service `api/track.js` adds the total and the payment's state for orders paid by transfer.

**Office: taking a step back.** "Undo delivered" and "Undo collected" (`store.undoStep(orderId, { reason, by })`) take back the
last collection or delivery a driver marked by mistake: the stop is open again, the order goes back to out for delivery (or on the
way to collect), and what that step recorded goes with it: its charges are voided, cash on delivery is pending again (not once it is
with the office), an invoice it sent goes back to pending, the collection photos come off the packages (they stay on file). Then
reschedule or reassign as usual. An order out for delivery can be reassigned: the new driver takes it out again from "collected".

**Bulk orders.** Every package still on its way can change driver (a failed one for its next attempt), and "Change driver" moves
everything still with one driver to another, for a driver changed in the middle of a distribution or the next morning's run.

**Office: details.** Postal and courier orders have "Edit collection details" (the carrier's own fields; a new Pikpost location or
post office moves the collection). Operators open business accounts directly ("New business account": name, contact, mobile, an
optional sign-in email, rates), and "Edit details" on an account sets the email whoever signs in to the business portal with gets
the account (the live service links it on sign-in).

**One package card for business orders.** The business portal's single order and the office's "New business order" fill the
package the way the bulk order does: receiver, number, delivery method and its details, size category with its rate, cash to
collect, instructions, reference and contents, then how many of the same, fragile and when (`MDM.bizKit.rowFields`; the card's
styles are in css/base.css). The address on the island is required for a business boat order.

**Request form.** Boat collection replaces administrative services (their older orders keep the label): the boat, the jetty it
arrives at, the arrival date and time and who hands it over; the jetty is the collection point. The order of the choices is home,
boat, shop or godown, courier. A boat delivery offers the customer's recent boats and receivers ("Send it like before").
