# Updates of 16/11/2026 (client points of 14/11/2026)

**Another receipt from the shop adds to the total.** The office added a second receipt to an order as two "Other" rows: "2nd Receipt"
MVR 190 and, worked out by hand, "Shopping fee 10%" MVR 19. The price table now has its own row for it.

- On a shopping order whose first receipt is in (the driver's, or the office's on the driver's behalf), the table has an
  **Add receipt** button beside "Add row". It adds a row "Receipt 2 at" with the shop's name: what it is for (optional), its total,
  and "Add a photo of the receipt" (optional). The × takes it off. An order with several shops has a list in the row to choose the shop.
- The receipts count together as the items bought, so the **shopping fee row follows by itself**: receipts of MVR 465 and MVR 190
  are MVR 655 of items and a fee of MVR 66 (10%), as the client worked it out (47 + 19).
- The order's Pricing section shows one row, "Receipt total" MVR 655, with "2 receipts: MVR 465 + MVR 190" under it; the shopping
  fee reads "of the receipts". Route shows "Receipts MVR 465 + MVR 190" on the shop stop and the photo of each receipt.
- The customer sees the same on the tracking page, the invoice and the payment page: "Shopping receipt", "2 receipts: MVR 465 +
  MVR 190", MVR 655.
- "Price changes" keeps it: "added receipt MVR 190 at" the shop, "receipt at … MVR 190 to MVR 200", "removed receipt …".
- Before the first receipt there is nothing to add to: the agreed cost of the items is typed as before. When the office takes the
  collection back ("Undo collected"), the added receipts go with the first one and the shopping is recorded again.
- A shopping order paid upfront and already delivered: "Shopping balance" (to refund or to collect) follows the new total.

In the data: `shop.extraReceipts: [{ id, amount, note, photoId, at, by }]` on the package's shop or the shop stop;
`store.confirmPrice(id, { items: { …, receipts: [{ id, shop: 'main' | <collection id>, amount, note, photoId }], knownReceipts } })`;
`MDM.pricing.receiptsNote(order)`.

**Every charge is billed to the customer by default.** "Keep all charges billing to customer as default and option to stop billing to
customer." Before, packing and Maldivian Cargo charges were billed, naalu followed a setting (off), and vehicle and other charges
were not billed.

- Naalu a driver records at collection or at delivery, and any charge the office adds (naalu, vehicle, packing, Maldivian Cargo,
  other), is added to the customer's price when it is recorded. In the order's Charges it reads "Billed to the customer" with
  **Stop billing the customer** beside it; a charge that was stopped reads "Not billed" with "Bill to the customer".
- "Add charge": "Bill to the customer" is ticked for every kind of charge; untick it when we pay it ourselves.
- One exception, so nobody pays for a vehicle twice: what was paid for an extra vehicle ("Add extra vehicle charge" → "Paid for the
  vehicle") is not billed by itself, because the customer pays the extra vehicle charge of the price. In Charges it reads "In the
  extra vehicle charge". For the same reason, adding a "Vehicle" charge to an order whose price already has an extra vehicle charge
  unticks the box, and the dialog says why.
- Settings → Payment no longer has "Charge naalu to the customer by default": it is always the default now.
- The driver's and the office's forms say under the naalu field that it goes on the customer's bill.
- Charges recorded before this update stay as they were: one that reads "Not billed" can be billed with its button.
