# Web app updates 26/10/2026

Client edits sent on 25/10/2026, with screenshots. Weekly billing is in UPDATES-2026-10-26-billing.md.

**Shop & Deliver without a budget.** The customer names the shop (optional: empty means "Any shop", we choose), the area, the
shop phone and the items. No shop address, landmark, budget or "if something is not available" choice. Our team calls the
customer to agree the items and their cost before buying. The office enters that cost per shop in Confirm price
(`MDM.store.confirmPrice(id, { itemCosts: { main | <collection id>: MVR }, delivery })`): it is stored as `shop.budget` (now "the
agreed cost", the receipt still replaces it), the shopping fee follows it, and `delivery` sets the rest of the price. Until then the
estimate is the delivery only and every page shows "Items and shopping fee: agreed on a call before we buy". `MDM.orderShops(o)`
lists the shops on an order; `MDM.shopName(shop)` reads "Any shop" for an unnamed one. Shop stops on a multi-collection order
follow the same rules (no address needed; `createOrder` keeps a shop stop that has a list or a name).

**Packages per stop.** Step 3 counts Bag / Box / XL for each stop when there are extra stops. A package collected at an extra stop
carries `collectionId` (the stop's `extraCollections[].id`, made in the form); `buildStops` gives those packages to that stop, so
the driver sees "Collect 2 × Box" on the stop and must tick "I have 2 × Box from this stop" in the Collected dialog when the order
has more than one collection. Orders from before keep every package on the first collection.

**Couriers.** The carrier list is grouped: Post and lockers (Redbox Maafannu, Redbox Main office, Pikpost, Post Office), then
Couriers (DHL, each courier on its own, Other courier). The office edits the couriers in Rates → Couriers (`settings.ops.couriers`:
`{ name, address, zone }`, up to 16; defaults FedEx, CPEX, PDU, UPS, Aramex). Orders still save `carrier: 'courier'` with
`details.courierName`.

**Wording and pages.** Express note "Express depends on availability. We confirm it with the price."; no size descriptions in
step 3; footer "Pick, Shop, Deliver across Greater Malé."; landing: Shop & Deliver as four points, no prices anywhere, the list
mock-up shows iPhone 18 Pro / TCL 55″ TV / Huawei Smart Watch, weekly billing with the due days from settings, coverage list
"Resort counters & terminals", Villimalé like the others, and the map names the four places without outlines; business page:
no rates section. No grocery names on any page or in the demo data (settings rev 2 drops "food" from the bag size guide).

**Home screen.** Every page has `apple-touch-icon` (assets/apple-touch-icon.png, the favicon's rider on full-bleed red) and
`apple-mobile-web-app-title` "Mr. Delivery Man"; the home page links `manifest.webmanifest` (192, 512 and maskable icons).
