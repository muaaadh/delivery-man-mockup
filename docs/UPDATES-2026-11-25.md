# Updates of 25/11/2026 (client points of 21 to 24/11/2026)

## Orders late in the evening

"Last night after 11pm, couldn't record any new order in system."

- From 23:00 a delivery about an hour later falls after midnight. The order form read it as a time earlier today and refused it
  ("Choose a time later today…"); the business order forms refused it as "Delivery must be after collection". An order without
  times was saved.
- Now each time has its own day. In the evening a time after midnight is tomorrow's, and the line under the field says so
  ("Tomorrow, 21/11"). The day is kept from the moment it is typed, so a form sent after midnight means the same.
- Our team and a business may also record a time that has passed (an order entered afterwards); the line says "Already passed
  today". A customer is still asked for a later time.
- A delivery earlier than its collection is refused only when it really is, and the message names both times.
- A business order's Advance timing has a delivery date and time ("Collect tonight, deliver tomorrow at 10:00").
- A time typed without AM or PM says "Finish the time" instead of counting as empty.
- The footer's "Closed now · opens 9am" no longer shows on the office's own order form.

On the live service, the pages also cope better when Supabase cannot be reached (below): its status page lists a disruption from
23:00 to 23:20 that night. Which of the two it was is not proven.

## The office's New order form is one page

"Is there any chance, you can turn these 6 pages into 1 or 2 pages?"

- request/?office=1 shows everything at once: the customer, Service, Collection, Packages, Delivery, Updates to the customer, Price
  and payment. Two columns on a laptop, one on a phone. The estimate and **Create order** stay at the bottom of the screen.
- Create order marks everything that needs attention, says how many, and goes to the first. Enter in a field does not create the order.
- "New order" always opens an empty form; a reload keeps what is being typed; **Clear form** empties it.
- Customers keep the six short steps. The office can still open them with request/?office=1&steps=1.

## Photos of a collection or a delivery

"Can do an option to remove or update the uploaded collection/delivery picture?"

- **Remove** (with a reason) on a collection, delivery or failed-attempt photo under "Photos and documents": it comes off the order
  and the tracking page and the file is deleted. **Replace** puts the right one in its place, without undoing the step.
- **Undo collected / Undo delivered** has a tick: also remove the photo of this step. Without it the old photo stays on file, marked
  "From a step that was taken back".
- The driver is told when a step is taken back, and the job says why and to take a new photo. The retake itself worked before.
- Receipts and bills that an amount rests on, transfer slips and the customer's own photos are not removed this way. The one photo
  of a whole bulk order comes off the one order and stays for the others.

## Package information after booking

"After placing the orders, sometime we have to update some package information."

- **Edit** on each package of an order (until it is delivered): what it is and fragile; on a business order also the package size,
  the reference and the cash to collect. The label, the driver and the tracking page follow. Another size changes the price of that
  package only. The driver is told; a business is told when its cash to collect changes.
- **Remove** on a package still to collect, when the order has others. The price goes down by what its row showed.
- Instructions changed in "Modify order" now reach the label and the driver.
- "Change category" prices only the package that changed (it used to price every package again at today's rates).

## Delete order

- **Delete order** (with a reason) in the order: for one recorded by mistake, as a test or twice. It leaves the board, the lists, the
  reports, the driver's jobs, the portal and its tracking link, and is kept under Orders, List, **Deleted** with who, when and why.
  **Restore** brings it back as it was.
- Only while nothing was collected, paid, charged or invoiced; the button says why when it cannot. Such an order is cancelled.
- The customer is not told; the driver it was given to is. A deleted order cannot be changed until it is restored.

## In the data

- `schedule.collectDay` / `schedule.deliverDay` beside `schedule.day`; `MDM.todayTimes(times, { now, lenient })`.
- `store.removePhoto(orderId, { photoId, reason })`, `store.undoStep(orderId, { reason, removePhotos, photoIds })`,
  `MDM.photoRemovable`, `MDM.photoOnOrder`, `MDM.stepPhotoIds`.
- `store.editPackage(orderId, packageId, { description, fragile, tier, reference, cod }, { by, remarks })`,
  `store.removePackage(orderId, packageId, { reason })`, `MDM.packageRemovable`.
- `store.deleteOrder(orderId, { reason })`, `store.restoreOrder(orderId)`, `MDM.deleteRefusal(order)`, `MDM.deletedReason(order)`;
  `order.deleted = { at, by }` (the reason is in the internal timeline). Reads leave deleted orders out:
  `list('orders', { deleted: true })` lists them, `get('orders', id, { deleted: true })` reads one.

## On the live service

- No migration. Five new calls: `removePhoto`, `editPackage`, `removePackage`, `deleteOrder`, `restoreOrder`, all the office's.
- The generic `remove` takes files only (an office sign-in could take any order out of the database before), and a driver can no
  longer remove the proof of a collection or delivery they took.
- `api/track.js`: a deleted order has no tracking page.
- When Supabase cannot be reached, a page keeps the sign-in, says "We cannot reach the server. Trying again…" and goes on when it
  answers; "Wrong username or password" only when the password is wrong; a save without an answer ends after 25 s and says to check
  the order list first; the API answers 503 "try again" instead of "Your session has ended". A real refusal still ends at the sign-in.
- The function log has one line per refused call: method, status, code. Never names, numbers or addresses.
