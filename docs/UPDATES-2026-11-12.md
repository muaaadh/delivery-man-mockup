# Updates of 12/11/2026 (client points of 11 and 12/11)

**Orders as a board.** The office's Orders page opens on a board with five columns, as in the sketch: **New order**, **Dispatch**, a slim
**Advance / Office** column, **Delivery** and **Payment**. "Board" and "List" at the top switch between it and the list as it was (the
queue tabs, the table, Export CSV); each person's choice is kept. A link into a queue (Overview, a customer's or a business's orders, a
bulk order) still opens the list. Search and Filters work on both.

Each card shows the order number with the time that matters now (top right, red once it has passed, amber within the hour or on an
urgent order), the customer and their number with how they talk to us (WhatsApp in green, Viber in purple, from "Notify by" or how the
order came in; a tap opens the chat), Express or the priority, Business, the collection address with its time, the packages
("1 × Bag · 2 × Box"), the delivery address with its time, and the driver. A tap on the card opens the order as before.

The coloured button at the bottom is the step the order is at, with the next step beside it; pressing it does that next step and the
card moves on:

| Column | Steps |
|---|---|
| New order | New order (red), Confirmed (red), Assigned (yellow), then Dispatch moves it on |
| Dispatch | Dispatched (blue), On the way (yellow), Collected (green), then Out for delivery moves it on |
| Delivery | Out for delivery (yellow), Delivered (green), then Request payment moves it on |
| Payment | Payment requested (yellow), Due (red), Slip received (blue), Paid (green) |

- A new order without a driver has a driver list on the card: choosing a driver assigns them. A driver's name on a running order changes
  the driver. Steps that need details (confirm the price, collected, delivered, paid) open the same dialog as in the order.
- A delivered order whose price is confirmed sends its invoice on delivery (as before), so it goes straight to Payment.
- **Due**: a payment asked for more than a day ago and not paid. Paid orders show for the day they are paid; business orders on the
  weekly invoice show as Delivered for the day they are delivered.
- **Advance / Office**: advance orders for a later day (they move to New order on their collection day) and collected orders back at
  the office, with less detail. "Out for delivery" there asks for the driver and sends it out.
- A bulk order's packages waiting for the bulk collection, or sorted at the office, are one card per bulk order ("4 packages to
  collect"), which opens the bulk order.
- A failed order shows in Dispatch or Delivery (where it failed), in red, with "Decide".

**Urgent orders in time order, timings optional on normal orders.** On the request form (customer and office), the office's business
order and the business portal, a normal or express order can now have a collection time and a delivery time for the day. They are
optional on a normal order; an express order needs at least one of the two. Another day is still an advance order. A business's
preferred collection time counts as its collection time. Every board column, and the driver's jobs, are in this order: urgent first
(an approved urgent request, then another priority, then express), each by its time, soonest first (the collection time until it is
collected, then the delivery time); then the orders without a time, the oldest first. The driver sees the time on each job ("Collect
10:30", "Deliver by 14:00", red once passed), and the tracking page lists the times under the order's details.

**The confirmed price in the package's own row.** When our team confirms a different price, the package row now shows the confirmed
price (for example "Box · Malé to Hulhumalé Phase 2 · MVR 50"), with no "Price confirmation −MVR 10" row under it. This is the same in
the office's order, the tracking page, the invoice, the payment page, the business portal and new weekly business invoices; the office
still sees the estimate it replaced under "Price changes". Orders confirmed before today show the same way when the reason chosen was
"Price confirmation" (the default); from today any reason chosen in the confirm dialog does. Two exceptions keep their own row: an
extra vehicle charge, and a cut larger than the packages' price (rare). Adjustments added with "Add adjustment" stay rows of their own. On an order with several packages the difference
goes on the first package, and a cut larger than its price carries on to the next.

**Tracking page.** The map is now the last section, after Activity. "Photos and documents" moved up between Progress and "Your
driver". Tapping a stop at the top brings the map into view on it.
