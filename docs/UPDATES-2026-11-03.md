# Updates of 03/11/2026 (client list of 02 and 03/11)

**Live fix first (pushed 02/11).** On the live service "Undo delivered / collected" and "New business account" ran only in the open
office page (`undoStep` and `createBusinessAccount` were missing from `js/remote.js` WRITES): MDM-1185 stayed delivered on the server,
hence "This order is already delivered" on Mark delivered, and new accounts were gone on the next load. Both are saved now;
`tools/supabase/test-r8.js` checks that the browser sends every API method, and test-live section 12 reads both back from the database.

**Driver: fewer fields.** "Delivery problem" asks only "What happened" (required), with an optional photo; it is saved as reason
`other` and shown as "Delivery failed" with the driver's words (`MDM.failReasonLabel`). "Delivered" has no "Handed to" and no
"received it" tick: the proof of delivery photo comes first and is required for every delivery (business ones too), "Received by" is
filled in, and the charges stay folded. On the live service the API refuses a driver's delivery without a photo of that order. The
office's "Mark delivered" asks the same two things, the photo optional there.

**A missing photo without undoing.** The order's route shows "Add photo" on a collection or delivery done without one
(`store.addStopPhoto(orderId, stopId, { photoId, by })`): the office adds the photo the driver sent, the order stays where it is.

**Bulk orders: the driver collects with a checklist.** "Assign collector" names the driver who collects a bulk order from the
business (`store.assignCollector(batchId, driverId)`; the driver is told). It shows on that driver's phone under "Bulk collections":
where, who, the packages, and "Collect" with a tick per package, a photo (required) and a note for anything missing
(`batchCollected` with `photoId`). The ticked packages wait at the hub without a driver; each zone of the bulk order has "Driver for
this zone" to give them out (and "Mark collected" in the office takes an optional photo). On the live service the collector reads
the bulk order and its packages still to collect (migration `20261103000100_bulk_collector.sql`).

**Request form: a collection type per stop.** "Collect an item" at stop 2 and on asks Home, Boat, Shop or godown, or Courier, with
that type's details (the boat and the jetty, the shop and its invoice number, the courier and its code). The stop's address and
contact follow from them, and its instructions start with the type for the driver; `extraCollections[]` keep `type` and `details`.

**Order received by.** Phone red, WhatsApp green, Viber purple (a dot, and the whole choice once chosen); Walk-in as it was.

**Petty cash.** A new page (Accounts → Petty cash) lists every amount paid out for orders by kind (Naalu, Vehicle, Maldivian Cargo,
Packing, Other), driver and period: what is still to pay back to each driver with "Pay back" (a petty cash voucher or reference), what
was paid back and what the office paid, totals by kind, and a CSV. The order's charges have "Pay back" next to "To pay back to the
driver". A vehicle paid for is a charge of its own (charge type `vehicle`; the extra vehicle charge asks what was paid and by whom).

Tests: `tools/_scratch/r9-client.js` (demo); live `tools/supabase/test-r9.js`, test-live section 12.
