# Updates of 31/10/2026 (client screenshots)

**Postal collection.** Redbox (Maafannu) and Pikpost ask for the SMS collection code and the collect-before time first; the
tracking number is optional for them (every other carrier still asks for it). Pikpost has a new location, "Post Building, ground
floor" (`post_building` in `MDM.PIKPOST_LOCATIONS`).

**Shop or godown collection.** An invoice shows the goods are paid for, so it is uploaded alone; only a quotation asks for the
payment slip as well. The review and the office show "Invoice" or "Quotation and payment slip".

**Jetty not known.** Every harbour or jetty list (request form, business portal, Excel import, office and driver boat dialogs)
ends with "Not sure, kindly assist in checking" (`unsure` in `MDM.geo` terminals; the client's wording, with a comma instead of a
dash). It is priced and mapped as Malé; the hand-over reads "Jetty to be confirmed", the driver sees "Not confirmed yet, ask the
office" and the office drawer flags it until the boat details are updated. "Contact on the island" is now "Contact of the boat"
(the boat's `contact`); the driver sees it as "Boat contact" when it is not the receiver.

**Order drawer.** A delivered order still to be paid has "Send update" and "Request payment" (or "Verify payment" once a slip is
in) in the footer next to Labels. Send update on such an order states the amount due.

**New business order (office).** The drawer runs Account, Collection, Delivery (the method), then "Receiver and packages" (the
receiver with each package and its size category), then the boat, Maldivian Cargo or airport details, then the price. In the
package form each size shows the account's rate for its category (it showed the old flat business rate, MVR 25, for every
size); the "Under 1 ft" flat-rate tick is gone there, the size sets the category.

**A photo of each package at collection.** The driver's Collected dialog asks a photo and a category per package (a stop with no
package of its own, an extra collection, keeps one photo). `store.setStop(…, { status: 'done', packagePhotos: { packageId: fileId } })`
keeps `stop.proofPhotoIds` (all, in package order), `stop.proofPhotoId` (the first, for older readers) and
`package.collectionPhotoId`. The office drawer shows every photo on the stop and each package's own; the tracking page lists them.

**Bulk collection with packages missing.** "Mark collected" on a bulk order lists the packages awaiting collection, all ticked;
the office unticks what the business did not hand over. `store.batchCollected(batchId, { orderIds, note })` collects the ticked
ones; the others stay awaiting collection with the public event "Not received at collection", the business gets a notification
and the bulk order keeps its Mark collected button for them.

**Driver sign out.** Office phones are shared by both shifts, often from the home-screen app. The driver console has "Sign out"
at the top (and at the bottom, where "Switch driver" was): the driver goes offline, the sign-in ends and the login page opens for
the next driver. The demo returns to "Who is driving?".

**Another account in another tab (live service).** "Mark collected" on BLK-1001 answered "Only the office can do this": the browser
keeps one sign-in for every tab, so signing in to the business portal (or out, or as a driver) in another tab changed the account
the open office page was writing as. `js/remote.js` now notices that the session is no longer the one the page opened with and
reloads the page, which then follows the new sign-in (the office signs in again).
